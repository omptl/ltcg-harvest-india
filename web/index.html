tax-harvest

Sec 112A LTCG redemption planner for Indian retail MF investors. Runs entirely in your browser.

🔒 100% client-side. Your CAS PDF, password, and broker P&L are processed in your browser via WebAssembly Python (Pyodide). Nothing is uploaded, logged, or sent to any server. View source on GitHub →
⚠ Educational tool, not investment or tax advice. This is automated arithmetic over your own statement. Verify with a Chartered Accountant before transacting. NAV at execution will differ from the NAV shown in the plan. Tax rules reflect the Indian Income Tax Act as understood at build time (post-Budget 2024).
